# Coverage Policy: MRI Lumbar Spine Without Contrast

**Service Code**: CPT 72148
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Superseded Date**: None (current)
**Policy ID**: RAD-072148-v3

## Coverage Criteria

MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast is covered when **all** of the following
are met:

1. Documented low back pain, with or without radiculopathy, present for at least
   6 weeks, AND
2. Documented completion of a trial of conservative therapy (physical therapy,
   NSAIDs, or chiropractic care) of at least 6 weeks duration with inadequate
   response, AND
3. A physician progress note dated within 30 days of the request

## Expedited Exception (Red Flag Criteria)

The 6-week conservative therapy requirement is **waived** if any of the following
red flag findings are documented:

- New bowel or bladder dysfunction (suspected cauda equina syndrome)
- Progressive motor weakness
- Suspected spinal infection or malignancy (history of cancer, fever, IV drug use)
- Significant trauma with suspected fracture

In these cases the request should be processed as **expedited** regardless of the
conservative therapy history.

## Required Documentation

- Physician progress note (within 30 days)
- Documentation of conservative therapy trial and duration (unless red flag
  criteria apply)
- Clinical indication for the study

## Exclusions

- Routine screening in the absence of symptoms is not covered
- Repeat MRI within 6 months of a prior study for the same indication requires
  documentation of significant clinical change
