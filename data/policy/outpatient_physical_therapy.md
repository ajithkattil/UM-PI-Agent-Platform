# Coverage Policy: Outpatient Physical Therapy

**Service Code**: CPT 97110
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Superseded Date**: None (current)
**Policy ID**: REHAB-097110-v2

## Coverage Criteria

Outpatient physical therapy is covered without prior authorization for the first
20 visits per plan year, provided:

1. A physician or qualified provider referral is on file, AND
2. A documented plan of care specifying the functional limitation being treated

Prior authorization is required for visits **beyond the initial 20** in a plan
year. Requests for additional visits require **all** of the following:

1. Progress notes from the completed visits showing functional improvement or
   documented plateau requiring continued skilled intervention, AND
2. An updated plan of care with specific, measurable functional goals, AND
3. Physician re-certification within the prior 30 days

## Required Documentation (for visits beyond 20)

- Progress notes covering the visits used to date
- Updated plan of care
- Physician re-certification note

## Exclusions

- Maintenance therapy with no expectation of functional improvement is not
  covered beyond the initial 20 visits
- Requests lacking progress notes from prior visits should be denied citing
  insufficient documentation of continued medical necessity, not automatically
  escalated
