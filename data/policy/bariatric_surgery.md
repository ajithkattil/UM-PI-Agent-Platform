# Coverage Policy: Bariatric Surgery (Roux-en-Y Gastric Bypass)

**Service Code**: CPT 43644
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Superseded Date**: None (current)
**Policy ID**: SURG-043644-v4

## Coverage Criteria

Bariatric surgery is covered when **all** of the following are met:

1. BMI of 40 or greater, OR BMI of 35–39.9 with at least one documented
   obesity-related comorbidity (type 2 diabetes, hypertension, obstructive sleep
   apnea, or cardiovascular disease), AND
2. Documented completion of a physician-supervised weight management program of
   at least 6 months duration within the 2 years prior to the request, AND
3. Psychological evaluation clearing the member for surgery, with no
   contraindicating findings, AND
4. Documentation that the member has been informed of and understands the
   procedure's risks and required post-operative lifestyle changes

## Medical Necessity Criteria

The comorbidity, when relied upon for a BMI of 35–39.9, must be clinically
significant and actively managed — not a historical diagnosis with no current
treatment. Reviewers should verify the comorbidity documentation reflects active
management (current medications, recent relevant labs, or ongoing treatment)
rather than accepting a diagnosis code alone.

## Required Documentation

- Current BMI with height/weight measurement
- Comorbidity documentation (if BMI 35–39.9), including evidence of active
  management
- Physician-supervised weight management program records (duration, dates)
- Psychological evaluation
- Informed consent / risk discussion documentation

## Notes on Ambiguous Cases

Cases where the comorbidity is present but management status is unclear from the
submitted documentation should not be denied outright — this policy requires
verification of active management specifically, which often needs clarification
from the provider rather than a same-pass approve or deny decision.
