# Coverage Policy: Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) Supplies

**Service Code**: HCPCS A4239
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Superseded Date**: None (current)
**Policy ID**: DME-A4239-v5

## Coverage Criteria

CGM supplies are covered for members who meet **all** of the following:

1. Diagnosis of Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes mellitus, AND
2. Currently on an intensive insulin regimen (3 or more injections per day, or
   insulin pump therapy), AND
3. Documentation of self-monitoring blood glucose testing at least 4 times per
   day within the 30 days prior to the request, AND
4. Physician order specifying CGM is medically necessary for glycemic management

## Medical Necessity Criteria

CGM is considered medically necessary when the member has demonstrated one or
more of the following despite adherence to the intensive insulin regimen:

- Recurrent hypoglycemia (blood glucose < 70 mg/dL) documented on at least 2
  occasions in the prior 90 days
- HbA1c above target range despite regimen adherence
- Hypoglycemia unawareness documented by treating physician

## Required Documentation

- Physician order
- Diabetes diagnosis and type
- Insulin regimen details (injections per day or pump therapy)
- Self-monitoring glucose testing frequency and results for the prior 30 days
- Most recent HbA1c result, if available

## Notes on Ambiguous Cases

Members newly diagnosed or newly transitioned to intensive insulin therapy
(within the prior 30 days) may not yet have 30 days of self-monitoring data
available. These cases should be evaluated individually — this policy does not
specify an exception, and reviewers should use clinical judgment rather than a
strict documentation-completeness denial.
